ACADEMIC REGULATIONS AND REGISTRATION POLICIES
Document Ref: REG-2025/2026-REV4
Applicability: All Undergraduate and Postgraduate Programs

Section 1: Course Registration and Credit Limits
1.1 Standard Term Load:
- The standard credit registration limit for a regular semester (Fall / Spring) is between 16.0 and 24.0 credits.
- Minimum registration per term to maintain full-time student status is 12.0 credits.
- Underload (fewer than 12.0 credits) is only permitted in the graduating semester with Dean / Faculty Advisor written consent.
- Overload policy: A student with a CGPA >= 8.5 may register for an overload of up to 27.0 credits per semester with advisor approval.
- Freshmen / First-year students cannot exceed 20.0 credits in Semester 1 and Semester 2.

Section 2: Prerequisite and Co-requisite Policy
2.1 Hard Prerequisites:
- A student cannot register for an advanced course unless all designated prerequisite courses are successfully completed with a passing grade (Letter Grade 'D' or higher, unless explicitly noted otherwise).
- If a course specifies a prerequisite, no concurrent enrollment in the prerequisite and target course is permitted under any circumstances.
2.2 Co-requisites:
- A co-requisite course is one that MUST be taken either concurrently in the same semester or successfully completed in an earlier term.
2.3 Instructor Consent and Prerequisite Waivers:
- Prerequisite waivers are granted ONLY in exceptional cases (e.g., credit transfer from recognized partner universities or demonstrated equivalent competency).
- To request an exception or prerequisite override, the student must secure formal written consent from the Course Instructor, approval of the Head of Department (HOD), and submission to the Academic Registrar prior to the add/drop deadline.

Section 3: Grading Scheme and Passing Standards
3.1 Grading Scale:
- Letter grades S (90-100%, 10 pts), A (80-89%, 9 pts), B (70-79%, 8 pts), C (60-69%, 7 pts), D (50-59%, 6 pts), E (40-49%, 5 pts), F (Fail, 0 pts).
- Grade 'D' is the minimum passing grade for degree requirements and prerequisites unless specified by specific program guidelines.
- Grade 'F' indicates failure; credits are not earned, and prerequisites are not satisfied. The course must be repeated in a subsequent term.

Section 4: Course Availability and Scheduling
4.1 Semester Offerings:
- The Academic Catalog lists approved curriculum and prerequisite dependencies.
- Actual course availability, specific section schedules, classroom allocations, instructor assignments, and available seat quotas are determined on a semester-by-semester basis by the Registrar and published in the 'Schedule of Classes' prior to registration.
- If a course offering or instructor detail is not found in catalog documents, students must refer to the University Scheduling Portal or consult their Faculty Academic Advisor.

Section 5: Course Repeats and Grade Improvement
5.1 Repeat for Improvement:
- A student may repeat a course in which a grade of 'E' or 'F' was obtained.
- Maximum of 3 repeat attempts allowed per course across the entire degree tenure.
